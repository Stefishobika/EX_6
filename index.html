Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our ordering process.

How far in advance should I place custom orders? +
We recommend placing custom cake orders at least 48 to 72 hours in advance to guarantee availability and specific design requests.
Do you offer gluten-free or allergen-safe options? +
Yes! We offer specialized Gluten-Free and Nut-Free options. Look for the tags on our catalog menu. Note that all items are baked in a shared kitchen.
What is your delivery policy? +
We offer local courier delivery within a 15-mile radius for a flat $10 fee, or free store pickup during business hours.
